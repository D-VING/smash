# SMASH-CAU
A browser-based tactical card battle game.

## Play
Open [index.html](./index.html) in a modern browser. To publish it with GitHub Pages, go to **Settings → Pages → Deploy from a branch → main / root**.

## Current build
- VS Computer: play against a basic strategy AI.
- Local Duel: two players take turns on the same device.
- Three preset decks: Balanced, Rush, Control.
- Four fighters with unique character abilities and passive effects.
- Attack, healing, defense, combo, lifesteal, burn and ultimate cards.
- Animated card hover, fighter hit/heal feedback, health bars and battle log.

## Controls
1. Choose a mode, preset deck and fighter.
2. Play cards by clicking them; the cost is shown in the upper-right corner.
3. Use your fighter's unique ability once per battle.
4. End your turn to pass play or let the AI respond.

This is an early playable build. Online multiplayer, deck editing, sound and additional card content can be added in later updates.


## Other game: SMASH WORLD
A separate 3D destruction sandbox is available in [games/smash-world](./games/smash-world/). Choose a generic school or city and use bombs, a baseball bat, laser, meteor, earthquake and a nuclear blast. Includes synthesized sound effects and camera controls.
